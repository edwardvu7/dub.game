Settings

dub.game speaks with your own ElevenLabs and Gemini accounts. Keys are encrypted on this computer and never leave it.

  1. Click Get a key and create a key on each site. Both have a free plan.
  2. Paste each key here and click Save.
  3. Click Test. A green check means that service is ready.
