FAQ

dubplay scans your Steam library and puts a small hook script into a game when you switch Voices on. The hook sends each line of dialogue to the app on your PC, Grok picks a voice for each character, and ElevenLabs speaks the line. It's built for Windows, and you need an ElevenLabs key and an xAI (Grok) key.

Ren'Py, Godot and RPG Maker MV games work today, and the site marks all three as Beta. Unity and RPG Maker MZ show up in the list as coming soon. Godot support depends on how a game draws its text, so it varies from game to game.

It adds one small script, or for Godot a config entry, and nothing else in the game. Switch Voices off and the app removes what it added. On a Ren'Py game's first run it also starts the game windowed so the overlay can show, and after that your display choice sticks.

Keys are stored encrypted on your PC and never sent anywhere except to the provider they belong to. Casting and speech both call ElevenLabs and xAI, so dubplay needs an internet connection to voice new lines.

Yes. Press Shift+F2 in a game to open the overlay, then swap the voice or adjust stability, style and speed. A voice you set yourself is locked, so Grok won't overwrite it, and you can mute any character. Voices are saved per game.
