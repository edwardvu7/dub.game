Desktop Overlay & Engine Hook

Every unvoiced visual novel, fully voice acted in real time.

dub.game scans your Steam library, injects a lightweight dialogue hook, profiles every character once with Gemini, and speaks every line live with ElevenLabs.

View on GitHub How It Works
01 · Steam Scan

One-Click Hook

Automatically detects compatible Steam games. Tick a title to inject the hook—untick to remove it cleanly.

02 · Gemini Profiler

Auto Character Casting

Profiles each speaker once from dialogue context and maps them to a distinct voice ID, speed, and style.

03 · ElevenLabs TTS

Zero-OCR Dialogue

Reads exact speaker names and lines directly from the engine callback—no fragile screen capture required.

04 · Live Overlay

ReShade-Style Tuning

Press Shift + F2 in-game to open a translucent overlay and retune any character's voice mid-scene.
